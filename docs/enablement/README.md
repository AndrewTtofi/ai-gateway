# Enablement: one capability, three audiences

The same capability explained three ways, because the people who need it ask different
questions. The capability is **how AI tools (connectors) are governed**: an assistant or
agent that can look things up in other systems, and what stops it doing more than it should.

| For | Document | Their question |
|-----|----------|----------------|
| Compliance and risk | [One page](connectors-for-compliance.md) | What are the controls, and where is the evidence? |
| People who use the assistant (for example a trading desk) | [Quick guide](connectors-quick-guide.md) | What can I do, and what do I do when it says no? |
| Engineers | [Deep dive](connectors-deep-dive.md) | How does it work, and how do I add one? |
