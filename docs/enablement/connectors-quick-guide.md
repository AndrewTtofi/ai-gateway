# Using tools with the AI assistant

*A quick guide for people who use the assistant in their work, for example on a trading
desk or in a compliance team.*

## What tools are

The assistant can look things up for you in other systems instead of you copying and
pasting. Today:

| Tool | What it does | Who has it |
|------|--------------|-----------|
| Case lookup | The status and owner of a case, by its id | Compliance, trading desk |
| Pull requests | Open and recent code changes in a repository | Engineering |

Tools only read. None of them changes anything in the other system.

## What you need to do

Nothing. If your team has a tool, the assistant can use it when you ask something that
needs it ("what's the status of case C-1001?").

## When it says no

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
|--------------|---------------|-----------|
| "…needs a role it allows" | Your team doesn't have this tool | Ask your manager to request it from the platform team |
| "…is not eligible for 'confidential' data" | The tool can't be used with data marked that sensitive | Use it without the sensitive material, or ask the platform team which tool can |
| "Monthly budget … is exhausted" | You've reached this month's spending limit | Ask for more (below) |
| "This user is deactivated" or "not provisioned" | Your account isn't set up for the assistant | Contact the service desk |

## Asking for more budget

When you reach your monthly limit you can ask for an exception. Say how much and why; a
member of the platform team approves or declines it, usually the same day. If approved,
it lasts until the end of the month.

## What is recorded about you

That you used a tool, which one, and when. **Not** what you asked, what the answer was,
or which case you looked up.

## Three things to remember

1. **Mark sensitive work as sensitive.** The assistant routes confidential material only
   to models that are allowed to hold it. That depends on the work being labelled.
2. **A tool's answer is information, not an instruction.** Text that comes back from
   another system can be wrong, or written by someone else. Read it as you would an email.
3. **Access follows your team.** If you move teams, your tools change with you, straight
   away.
